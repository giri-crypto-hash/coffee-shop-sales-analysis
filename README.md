# coffee-shop-sales-analysis

**project background and over view**

Brew Haven Coffee Shop is a fictional coffee retail business established in 2019, offering a variety of coffee products to customers across different locations. As the business expands, analyzing sales performance, product preferences, and customer purchasing patterns becomes essential for improving business operations and supporting strategic decision-making.
Insights and recommendations are provided on following areas

**.Sales trend analysis:** Evolution of historical sales patterns both globally and by region focusing n revenue, order value and average order value
**.Product level performance:** An analysis of Brew Heaven's various product lines, understanding their impact on sales and returns
**.Loyalty programs success:** An assessment of the loyalty program on customer retention and sales
**.Regional comparison:** An evolution of sales and order by region
