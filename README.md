# coffee-shop-sales-analysis

### project background and over view

Brew Haven Coffee Shop is a fictional coffee retail business established in 2019, offering a variety of coffee products to customers across different locations. As the business expands, analyzing sales performance, product preferences, and customer purchasing patterns becomes essential for improving business operations and supporting strategic decision-making.
Insights and recommendations are provided on following areas

**Sales trend analysis:** Evolution of historical sales patterns both globally and by region focusing n revenue, order value and average order value.
**.Product level performance:** An analysis of Brew Heaven's various product lines, understanding their impact on sales and returns.                 
**.Loyalty programs success:** An assessment of the loyalty program on customer retention and sales.                               
**.Regional comparison:** An evolution of sales and order by region

### Executive summary
Coffee sales performance peaked in February 2020, generating the highest monthly revenue, before declining by 49.1% in March 2020, indicating significant month-over-month volatility. Annual revenue reached its highest full-year level in 2021, increasing by 13.6% compared with 2020. In February 2022, monthly revenue fell to a low of $393.63 before recovering by approximately 234.1% in March 2022. These fluctuations highlight the importance of monitoring monthly sales trends to identify peak periods, performance declines, and opportunities for revenue growth.

### sales trends 
Sales revenue remained relatively stable in 2020, declining by **0.6% compared with 2019**, before increasing by **13.6% in 2021** to reach $13,766.11. Monthly revenue peaked at **$1,798.34 in February 2020**, followed by a **49.1% month-over-month decline** in March. In 2022, revenue increased by **234.1% from February to March**, indicating a strong short-term recovery. As the dataset covers only January through August 2022, year-over-year comparisons should use the same period to ensure a fair assessment of sales performance.

### Product Performance
Arabica sales revenue demonstrated consistent growth, increasing by **14.7% in 2020** and a further **20.5% in 2021**, reaching a peak of $4,045.63. Liberica experienced the strongest recovery among coffee varieties in 2021, with revenue increasing by **47.3%** year over year to $3,836.70, following a 22.9% decline in 2020. In contrast, Excelsa revenue declined by **5.3%** and Robusta by **3.2%** in 2021, indicating weaker year-over-year performance. Revenue figures for 2022 are lower across all varieties; however, because the dataset covers only part of 2022, these figures should not be interpreted as a full-year decline.

### Regional Sales Analysis
Regional sales analysis indicates that the **United States contributed approximately 78.9% of total sales revenue**, making it the dominant market in the dataset. The remaining revenue was generated across other countries, highlighting an uneven geographic distribution of sales. This concentration presents an opportunity to evaluate customer demand in lower-contributing markets, identify potential growth regions, and develop targeted marketing strategies to support geographic expansion and more balanced revenue generation.

### Loyalty Programme Success
The loyalty programme analysis indicates that loyalty-card customers contributed **46.35% of total sales revenue**, while non-members accounted for **53.65%**. Loyalty members generated approximately **$20,917.85** in revenue across 464 distinct orders, compared with $24,216.40 across 493 orders from non-members. Average revenue per order was also lower among loyalty members ($45.08) than non-members ($49.12). These findings suggest that the programme's success is not yet evident from revenue contribution alone, highlighting an opportunity to evaluate member engagement, repeat purchases, and targeted incentives to improve programme effectiveness.

### recommemdationns
1. Market Diversification	The United States contributed 78.9% of total revenue ($35,638.89). Develop targeted marketing campaigns in other markets to reduce                                    dependence on a single country.
2. Product Strategy	Excelsa generated $12,306.44 (27.3% of revenue). Maintain product availability and test targeted promotions to sustain demand and improve sales further.
3. Roast-Type Optimization	Light-roast coffee generated $17,354.47 (38.5% of revenue). Prioritize inventory planning and promotional campaigns for this leading roast category.
4. Annual Sales Growth	Revenue increased from $12,117.55 in 2020 to $13,766.11 in 2021, representing 13.6% growth. Use the successful sales drivers from 2021 to develop future growth targets.
5. Monthly Sales Stability	Revenue fell 49.1%, from $1,798.34 in February 2020 to $914.79 in March 2020. Investigate the causes of monthly declines and introduce targeted promotions and demand-based inventory planning.
6. Loyalty Programme Optimization	Loyalty-card customers generated $45.08 per order, compared with $49.12 for non-members. Review the rewards structure and test personalized offers to increase average spending among loyalty members.
